# ERP Next — Revenue Cycle V1

## Leads
- LEAD-001 — Every inquiry has source, stage, owner and next action.
- LEAD-002 — Existing phone/customer identity is recognized before duplicate creation.
- LEAD-003 — Source attribution follows through booking, revenue and margin.

## Opportunities
- OPP-001 — Opportunities keep value, probability, expected close, owner and next action.
- OPP-002 — Technician recommendations can become opportunities without disappearing in notes.
- OPP-003 — Weighted pipeline feeds management forecasting.

## Estimates
- EST-001 — Estimates/proposals are versioned.
- EST-002 — Pricing and margin are visible before approval.
- EST-003 — Accepted proposals create governed operational handoff without duplicate entry.
- EST-004 — Out-of-policy discounts require elevated approval.

## Maintenance
- MAINT-001 — Maintenance is tied to customer, site and asset.
- MAINT-002 — Due windows use last-service date and configured interval.
- MAINT-003 — Recurring service reminders are consented operational communications.
- MAINT-004 — Inactive/drifting customers can surface as retention opportunities.

## Invoices
- AR-001 — Invoice and customer-level balances remain visible.
- AR-002 — Partial and aggregate payments do not hide remaining balance.
- AR-003 — Allocation priority: explicit reference → exact combination → oldest-invoice suggestion → human review.
- AR-004 — Aging and collection state remain connected to supporting evidence.

## Expenses
- EXP-001 — Voice can propose purchase line items; supplier invoice/receipt is monetary evidence.
- EXP-002 — Controlled Item Master drives classification.
- EXP-003 — One reviewed purchase can create expense, inventory/tool receipt, evidence archive and QBO sync intent.
- EXP-004 — Budget pace compares actual spend with elapsed time and forecasts month-end risk.

## Integration state
All surfaces use structured preview data. Persistence, live customer identity, real pricing, invoice sync and document extraction will be connected during the Firebase/QBO/integration phase.
