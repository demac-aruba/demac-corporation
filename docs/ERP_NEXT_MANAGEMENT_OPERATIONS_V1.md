# ERP Next — Management Operations V1

## Workforce
- HR-001 — Employee identity, role, team, status and skills are operational data.
- HR-002 — Dispatch can use skill/capacity data; payroll privacy remains separate.
- HR-003 — Weekday and Saturday work schedules are explicit.
- HR-004 — Overtime after 17:00 is tracked for accounting review.
- HR-005 — Employee self-view and management/payroll views remain permission-separated.
- HR-006 — Utilization and skill-gap intelligence can surface management risks.

## Projects
- PRJ-001 — Commercial/VRF projects have stage, budget, committed cost, forecast and margin.
- PRJ-002 — Project control follows scope/WBS through commissioning and handover.
- PRJ-003 — Procurement and serialized receiving can gate job readiness.
- PRJ-004 — Project inventory reservations remain linked to the project.
- PRJ-005 — Installed equipment becomes CRM asset history at handover.
- PRJ-006 — Forecast-at-completion exceptions are visible before closeout.

## Reports
- RPT-001 — Reports reconcile to governed source transactions.
- RPT-002 — Reports are role-specific.
- RPT-003 — Exception reporting is prioritized over manual hunting.
- RPT-004 — Standard reporting covers executive, operations, finance, inventory, communications and projects.
- RPT-005 — Scheduled cadence is part of report governance.
- RPT-006 — Future PDF/Excel exports must use the same governed calculations shown in the ERP.

## Integration state
Preview/interaction layer only. Live persistence and permission enforcement will be connected during the Firebase/auth integration phase.
